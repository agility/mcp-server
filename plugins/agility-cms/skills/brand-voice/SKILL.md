# Brand voice

## Find the guidelines

Use the organization's own guidelines. Look, in this order, for:

1. a guidelines document the user attached or pointed to in this conversation
2. a brand or style guide stored in their Agility instance (search for "brand",
   "style guide" or "voice" with `search_instance`)
3. their existing published copy: read three or four strong pages or articles
   and describe the voice you see (sentence length, person, formality, words
   they use and avoid)

If none of these exist, say so, describe the voice you will use, and ask the
user to confirm it before writing much.

Read the guidelines fresh each time. Do not rely on a version from earlier in
the conversation if the user may have changed it.

## Write

- Decide the one point the piece must make before drafting.
- Follow the guidelines' rules on person, tone, vocabulary, capitalization,
  punctuation and calls to action.
- Be specific. A line that could appear unchanged on a competitor's site is too
  generic; rewrite it with something only this organization can say.
- Do not state facts, numbers, rankings, prices, customer names or quotes that
  you cannot verify from the user's material. Flag them instead.
- Do not disparage competitors.

## Check before handing it over

Run the guidelines' own checklist if they have one. Otherwise check:

- Does it follow every explicit rule in the guidelines?
- Does it lead with what the reader gets?
- Is every claim supported by the user's material?
- Are headlines and calls to action consistent with the rest of the site?

## Report like an editor

When reviewing someone's copy, start with what works, then what to change and
why, quoting the guideline that applies. Criticize the copy, not the writer.
Give a revised version, and list anything you flagged for the user to verify.
